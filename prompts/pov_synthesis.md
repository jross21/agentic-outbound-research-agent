# Task: synthesize the point of view

From the evidence ledger, construct the "why-you / why-now" point of view that the outbound sequence will be built on. Follow the Outbound Playbook below — especially the why-you / why-now test and the grounding rule.

- **whyYou**: one or two sentences tying a specific, cited fact about this account (their model, stage, stack, or motion) to a specific reason our product is relevant. "You're a SaaS company" is not a why-you. Connect a real fact to a real reason.
- **whyNow**: one or two sentences anchored on the strongest recent timing signal in the ledger (funding, a key hire, a launch, a leadership change). No invented urgency.
- **claims**: the supporting points, each with `evidenceIds` referencing the ledger. Every claim must cite at least one real ledger id. Do not include a claim you cannot cite.

Lead with the priority the chosen persona owns. Keep it specific, true, and free of adjectives like "leading" or "innovative."
